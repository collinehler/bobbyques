About us

Bobby is originally from Florida and has been cooking for people since he was 14.

Bobby-Ques started as catering. When the pandemic hit, Bobby cooked meals and left them on people's doorsteps, no contact needed. Now Bobby-Ques has a home on Argyle Street in Waterloo, where you can pull up a chair and stay a while.

Let us cater your next event
